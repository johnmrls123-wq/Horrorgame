WHISPERING HOLLOW
A mobile descent into the dark forest

You wake among the trees with no memory of how you arrived. Find the five lost relics scattered through the haunted woods and reach the stone gate before the dark things find you. Your flashlight is your only friend — and its battery is dying.

LEFT JOYSTICK · MOVE RIGHT DRAG · LOOK BUTTONS · ACT
HOW TO PLAY

• Move — drag the analog joystick on the lower-left.
• Look — drag anywhere on the right half of the screen.
• Use — large button (bottom-right) to pick up relics & interact.
• Sprint — hold to run faster; drains stamina.
• Crouch — move quietly and lower your profile.
• Jump — hop over roots and debris.
• Light — toggle flashlight; it repels shadow figures but burns battery.

Collect all 5 relics and reach the stone gate to escape. Stay in the light. Trust nothing.
